Overview

StrokeMotion is designed as an offline handwriting, stroke editing and video creation app for iPad. This policy describes how the app handles information. The developer is [PUBLISHER_NAME].

Projects and creative content

Handwriting, strokes, images, project files and exported videos are processed on your device. The app is not designed to automatically upload these materials to a developer-operated server.

Accounts and information collection

The current release design does not require an account or operate a cloud-synchronization service. The developer does not intentionally collect users’ handwriting, project content, or personal profiles through an app backend. This statement must be reconfirmed if analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, cloud features or other network integrations are added.

Sharing and support

You control what you export or share using the iPad system sharing features. If you email support or voluntarily attach screenshots or diagnostic files, the information you send will be used to respond to your request and investigate the reported problem.

Apple services and diagnostics

App Store distribution, purchases, system backups and Apple diagnostics may be processed by Apple under its own policies and your device settings. The developer may receive limited diagnostic information through Apple developer tools when enabled by users.

Storage and deletion

App projects are stored locally. You can delete projects within the app where that function is available. Removing the app normally removes data in its app container; copies you made elsewhere or included in device backups may remain under those services’ controls.

Children and security

The app is not designed to solicit personal information from children. We aim to keep project data on-device and do not require online accounts for core functions.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. For privacy questions, contact [SUPPORT_EMAIL].
